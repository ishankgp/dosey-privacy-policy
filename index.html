dosey Privacy Policy

dosey is a local-first medication tracking app. For v1, your shot history, side effects, settings, and optional baseline details are stored on your device and are not sent to dosey servers.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app may store information you enter, including your first name or nickname, medication name, dose, injection schedule, shot history, injection sites, side effects, notes, optional weight, and optional height.

This data is stored in the app's local storage on your phone. dosey v1 does not create accounts, operate a production database, or sync this information across devices.

Sharing And Retention

dosey v1 does not sell, share, or transmit your stored tracking data to dosey servers. Your data remains on the device unless you choose to export it or your device backup settings copy app data elsewhere.

If you uninstall dosey, reset app storage, or clear app data, your locally stored data may be deleted from the device.

Deletion And Export

You can reset local app data from Settings. You can also export a local JSON copy of your dosey data from Settings before resetting or changing devices.

Health And Medical Note

dosey is for personal tracking only. It is not a medical device and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, dosing instructions, or emergency monitoring.
