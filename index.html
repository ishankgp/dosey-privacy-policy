Dosey Privacy Policy

Dosey is a local-first personal routine tracking app. For v1, the information you enter, your progress records, notes, and settings are stored on your device and are not sent to Dosey servers.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app may store information you enter, including your first name or nickname, routine schedule, logged entries, personal notes, optional progress readings, optional body measurements, and app settings.

This data is stored in the app's local storage on your phone. Dosey v1 does not create accounts, operate a production database, or sync this information across devices.

Sharing And Retention

Dosey v1 does not sell, share, or transmit your stored tracking data to Dosey servers. Your data remains on the device unless you choose to export it or your device backup settings copy app data elsewhere.

If you uninstall Dosey, reset app storage, or clear app data, your locally stored data may be deleted from the device.

Optional Feedback

If you choose to send a suggestion, Dosey opens a draft in your email app. Dosey does not automatically attach your stored routine entries, notes, progress readings, or settings.

Your feedback email and email address leave the app through your email provider. Avoid including sensitive personal details unless you intentionally choose to share them.

Deletion And Export

You can reset local app data from Settings. You can also export a local JSON copy of your Dosey data from Settings before resetting or changing devices.
